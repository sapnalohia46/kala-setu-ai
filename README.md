# Kala Connect

Build a polished, modern, mobile-first web application prototype for Smart India Hackathon 2026.

PROJECT NAME:

KalaSetu AI

TAGLINE:

From Craft to Market, Powered by AI

PROBLEM STATEMENT:

SIH26090 – AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Mobile Application for Marginalized Artisans.

CORE PURPOSE:

The platform should help marginalized artisans who may have limited digital literacy transform their handmade products into professional digital catalogues and intelligently connect them with relevant buyers and market opportunities.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT build this as another generic e-commerce marketplace.

The core innovation should be:

1. AI-powered smart cataloging

2. Multilingual and voice-first accessibility

3. AI-powered market opportunity matching

4. Buyer-to-artisan intelligent matchmaking

The experience should be extremely simple because many target users may not be comfortable with complicated digital interfaces.

Use high-quality placeholder images of Indian handicrafts such as pottery, textiles, jute products, wooden crafts and handmade décor.

Prioritize MOBILE-FIRST responsive design, but make desktop presentation polished because the prototype may be demonstrated to judges on a laptop.
